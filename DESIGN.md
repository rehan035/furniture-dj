# Artisanal Cane & Teak — Design System

This design system translates the heritage, patient craft, and tactile material culture of artisanal furniture making into a digital flagship for **Pakistan Rattan Furniture Atelier (Islamabad)**.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#27130B` / `#3E271E` - Deep Teak):** Aged timber tone providing grounded visual weight, structural typography, and primary interactive surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#885200` / `#C88637` - Natural Cane Amber):** Radiant golden-honey tone derived from freshly woven rattan peel.
- **Tertiary (`#071C0E` / `#1C3122` - Subdued Forest Evergreen):** Botanical accent inspired by the foothills of Islamabad.
- **Surface (`#FCF9F2` - Raw Linen Ecru):** Warm unbleached textile base that replaces stark white with calm residential comfort.
- **Surface Containers (`#F6F3EC`, `#F0EEE7`, `#EBE8E1`):** Tonal layering for cards, specifications, and architectural showcases.

## Typography

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Editorial heritage and bespoke luxury.
- **Body & Specifications (`DM Sans`):** Geometric clarity for dimensions, lead times, and commission details.
- **Tabular Metrics (`JetBrains Mono`):** Used for architectural dimensions, pricing, and commit SHAs.
