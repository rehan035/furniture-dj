## Brand & Style

This design system translates the heritage, patient craft, and tactile material culture of artisanal furniture making into a digital flagship. The aesthetic marries raw organic honesty with high-end editorial restraint, catering to discerning interior designers, architects, and homeowners seeking bespoke heirloom pieces. 

The visual language draws primarily from **Tactile Warmth** and **Minimalist Editorial Luxury**:
- **Tactile Materiality:** Evoking the textures of split-cane weave patterns, hand-planed teak, and natural slubbed linen. UI surfaces avoid sterile flatness in favor of subtle, organic depth and woven motif separators.
- **Architectural Poise:** Spacious layouts, serene pacing, and high deliberate breathing room mirror an airy, sun-drenched furniture atelier in Islamabad.
- **Craft Honesty:** Digital forms echo real-world joinery—structural clarity, refined proportional weight, and grounded balances.

## Colors

The color architecture directly mirrors natural raw workshop elements:
- **Primary (`#3E271E` - Deep Teak):** An aged, deep timber tone providing grounded visual weight, structural typography, and primary interactive surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C88637` - Natural Cane Amber):** A radiant, golden-honey tone derived from freshly woven rattan peel. Used deliberately for focal highlights, active states, and refined micro-details.
- **Tertiary (`#263B2B` - Subdued Forest Evergreen):** A botanical accent inspired by the lush foothills of Islamabad and raw foliage, offering a balancing, grounded counterpoint to the warm wood notes.
- **Neutral (`#F8F5EE` - Raw Linen Ecru):** A warm, unbleached textile base that replaces stark digital white with calm, residential comfort.

### Functional Palette Applications
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8F5EE` for main viewport backgrounds.
- **Layered Containers:** `#EFEAE0` for slightly recessed card backgrounds and structural surfaces.
- **Dividers & Weave Borders:** `#D9D0BE` with an option for 20% opacity overlays (`rgba(62, 39, 30, 0.12)`).
- **Text & High Contrast:** `#221812` for primary body copy (avoiding pure black `#000000` to preserve warmth).
- **Muted Text:** `#685A52` for metadata, captions, and secondary details.

## Typography

The typography couples the lyrical elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clear geometric utility of **DM Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys editorial heritage and bespoke luxury. Used for hero phrases, collection names, and editorial storytelling. Italic styling is welcomed for artisan quotes and material highlights (e.g., *Handwoven in Raw Cane*).
- **Body & Captions (DM Sans):** Balances the expressive serif with unpretentious clarity. Provides legible specifications, product dimensions, and narrative body copy.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered in uppercase DM Sans with generous letter-spacing (0.08em to 0.1em) to mimic luxury archival stamps and maker hallmarks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a structured fluid grid inspired by architectural blue-prints and artisan workbenches. Generous margins and breathing room frame furniture imagery like museum works.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content reaches a maximum container width of `1320px`.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Vertical gaps between editorial sections scale deliberately using `space-xl` and double-tier margins, reinforcing the deliberate unhurried luxury of handcrafted work.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** and **Ambient Earth Shadows**, strictly avoiding cold blue or gray drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation shifts are communicated through background values—moving from base `#F8F5EE` to surface container `#EFEAE0`, accented with `#E4DDCF` for elevated interactive panels.
- **Ambient Earth Shadows:** When floating or layering occurs (such as cart drawers, quick-view modals, or hover states), shadows are diffuse and warm:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(62, 39, 30, 0.05)`
  - *Elevated / Floating Card:* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(62, 39, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(62, 39, 30, 0.04)`
- **Woven & Inset Borders:** Structural definition relies on hairline borders (`1px solid #D9D0BE` or `rgba(62, 39, 30, 0.1)`). Where distinct craftsmanship is showcased, a subtle double-line border or dashed rattan stitch pattern may be applied.

## Shapes

The design system selects a **Soft (Level 1)** roundedness aesthetic.
- **Foundational Radius:** Standard interactive items (buttons, inputs, cards) use `0.25rem` (4px) to retain an architectural, hand-cut precision akin to joined woodwork.
- **Image Frames & Inner Panels:** Employ `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) to echo the soft curves of bent rattan poles and organic cane loops.
- **Chips & Pills:** Retain slight softening (`rounded-full` or `0.75rem`) solely for material swatches and tags, ensuring contrast against square structural blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Deep Teak (`#3E271E`) with Raw Linen Ecru text (`#F8F5EE`), 0.25rem border radius, and DM Sans medium/semi-bold uppercase typography. Hover transitions gently to Honey Amber (`#C88637`).
- **Secondary / Craft Outline:** Transparent surface with a 1px border of Deep Teak (`#3E271E`). Hover fills with `#EFEAE0`.
- **Tertiary / Botanical:** Forest Green (`#263B2B`) reserved for sustainability narratives, bespoke order actions, and custom consultation triggers.

### Chips & Material Tags
- Bordered with subtle `1px solid #D9D0BE`, filled with transparent or `#EFEAE0` backgrounds.
- Used for indicating timber species (*Seasoned Teak*, *Sheesham*), cane weaves (*Octagonal Weave*, *Close Mesh*), or stock availability (*Made to Order*).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background filled with `#FAF8F3`, featuring a bottom or full hairline border of `#D9D0BE`.
- On focus: Active border shifts cleanly to Deep Teak (`#3E271E`) or Cane Amber (`#C88637`) with no harsh glowing outline; focus rings use a soft 2px warm amber shadow.

### Cards & Furniture Showcases
- Built on `#EFEAE0` canvas surfaces with `0.25rem` to `0.5rem` outer corners.
- Image containers feature warm neutral backdrops (`#F2ECE1`) to let natural wood grains and cane textures take visual prominence without artifacting.
- Bottom card metadata pairs a Playfair title with DM Sans price, lead time, and woven material tags.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with softened corners (2px) or circular radios with a dual-ring cane accent on selection. Active states utilize `#3E271E` checks against `#C88637` accents.

### Specialized Artisanal Components
- **Weave Pattern Divider:** A stylized horizontal divider featuring a subtle 1px repeat SVG motif inspired by traditional Pakistani *charpai* and rattan cane webbing.
- **Swatch Selector:** Circular chips with real wood grain and rattan weave previews, highlighted by an outer ring in `#C88637` upon selection.
- **Lead Time Indicator:** A badge formatted as label micro-copy, communicating handcrafted production timelines (e.g., *Handcrafted: 3-4 Weeks*).
